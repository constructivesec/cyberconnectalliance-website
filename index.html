Connecting students, educators & the cybersecurity workforce

Helping students find their path into cybersecurity.

CyberConnect Alliance connects high school and college students with working cybersecurity professionals, so they can decide if the field is right for them and how to get started.

Our mission

We are not a job fair. We offer honest advice and guidance from people who do this work every day, so students can make informed choices about their education and careers.

Is a cybersecurity career right for me?
Which pathways and specialties fit my interests?
What education and certifications actually matter?
How do I approach the industry and get my first role?

What we do

Our programs bring students, educators, and industry professionals together.

Career forums

Fall and spring events with CISO and practitioner panels, career roadmaps, and time to talk with professionals one on one.

Capture the Flag

Hands-on, beginner-friendly security challenges with a live leaderboard. A fun first taste of real cybersecurity work.

Education pathways

Guidance on degrees, career colleges, and certifications, and how each maps to different roles in the field.

Industry connections

Partnerships with schools, employers, and public-sector organizations that open doors for students.

Inaugural event

Cyber Career Connections Forum

Our first student forum, with panels featuring cybersecurity leaders, career pathway sessions, and a student Capture the Flag competition.
